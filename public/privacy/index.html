MoneyQuest privacy policy

DRAFT for NHSQA to review. This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

MoneyQuest is a game that teaches personal finance to students in grades K–8. It can be played alone, with nothing leaving the device. Class features are optional and used through a school: the school or teacher authorizes students’ use and gives them a class code.

Playing without a class

The save file stays in the browser on that device. A nickname typed into the game is never sent anywhere. Checking a printed reward card code happens on the device.

We do not collect students’ real names, email addresses, birthdays, photos or location. Students cannot type anything other students can see: they choose emotes and phrases from a fixed list.

While a student is connected, the server also holds which screen they are on, where their character stands, their hat and pet, and their last 10 emotes and phrases. This is kept in memory only, for as long as they are connected, and is not stored unless a report is made.

Teachers

Only teachers create accounts. We store the teacher’s email address (used only as a login name; MoneyQuest never sends email), a scrambled form of their password, their sign-in times and their classes.

Technical data

Requests to our server include an IP address. Our server records an IP address only to slow down password and class-code guessing, and clears these records within about a day after they stop being needed.

How long data is kept

  • Classes with no activity for 12 months are deleted, with everything in them.
  • Reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Teacher accounts with no classes and no sign-in for 12 months are deleted.
  • A teacher can delete a class or remove a student at any time, and a student can leave a class. That deletes the student’s membership and progress summary right away. A report that is still open keeps the emotes and phrases it recorded until the report is deleted, but no longer links to that student.

Who else handles the data

Vercel hosts the game’s website. Cloudflare runs our server and database. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
