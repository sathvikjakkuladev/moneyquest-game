Notice for teachers

DRAFT for NHSQA to review. This is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

By creating a class you confirm that your school allows you to use MoneyQuest with your students. Check your school or district’s rules for approving online tools first.

What you see

  • Your students by public name only. Keep any note of who is who yourself: MoneyQuest never stores real names, so please don’t put real names in a name list.
  • Who is online, level, zones cleared, accuracy by topic, and Money Check scores with a class average and a CSV download.
  • Reports students make, with the reported student’s recent emotes and phrases.

What you control

  • Mute: turns off a student’s emotes and phrases for everyone. They can still play.
  • Remove: disconnects a student straight away and deletes their membership and progress summary.
  • Device code: moves a student to a new device and keeps their progress. Their old device is signed out.
  • Class settings: the weekly goal, hiding the leaderboard, and your list of public names.
  • Delete class: deletes the class and everything in it, for good.

What students can do

Students see classmates who are on the same screen. They can’t type anything other students can see: they pick from a fixed list of emotes and phrases. They can mute or report a classmate, leave the class, or switch on Play offline, which stops all class features and uploads until they switch it off.

Your account

Your email is only your login name; MoneyQuest never sends email. If you forget your password, ask the NHSQA admin to reset it. If you download the CSV, you are responsible for keeping that file safe.

How long data is kept

Classes with no activity for 12 months are deleted, along with everything in them. Reports are deleted after 90 days. There are no ads, analytics or trackers. The full details are in the privacy policy.
